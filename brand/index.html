One blue, in every depth

Klein Blue is the brand. Every other blue on our website, product, decks and emails is a shade or tint of it, so the whole brand reads as one colour with light and depth.

Colour

The Klein Blue scale

Eleven steps from Night to Tint. Klein Blue (600, outlined in yellow) is the brand colour on the logo, headers, primary buttons and links. Darker steps give depth, lighter steps give calm backgrounds.

Rules

How we use it

  • The logo is one flat colour: Klein Blue, white or black. No gradients, shadows or effects on the logo, print, QR codes or swag.
  • Glow and gradients belong to the website and product backgrounds only, always built from the Klein Blue scale.
  • No other hues for decoration. Depth comes from darker and lighter steps of Klein Blue, never from coloured accent bars.
  • Real photography only. People shown are illustrative and never presented as customers.
  • Say "AI". No long dashes and no emoji in anything we publish.
  • Text on Klein Blue or darker is white. Text on Tint or white is Ink. Secondary text is Muted.
