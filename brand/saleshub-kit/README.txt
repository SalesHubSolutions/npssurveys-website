SalesHub wordmark kit, November 2026

SalesHub is the company; NPS Survey is the flagship product. The company mark is a typed wordmark: "SalesHub" in Inter Bold, converted to outlines (no font needed). Brand colour: Klein Blue #002FA7.

Which file
- On white or light backgrounds: saleshub-wordmark-klein (or -black for one-colour print)
- On Klein Blue or dark backgrounds: saleshub-wordmark-white; -on-klein has the blue background built in
- With the product line: the -makers-of-nps-survey versions (use where people may not know SalesHub yet)
- Square avatars (LinkedIn, email signatures): saleshub-avatar-on-klein-800

Formats: svg (web, Figma, Canva, PowerPoint), pdf (print, vector), png (transparent, 2x).

Rules
- One flat colour. No gradients, shadows, outlines or effects.
- Clear space around it at least the height of the "a". Minimum width 90 px.
- Don't stretch, rotate, recolour, or retype it. Always "SalesHub" with a capital S and H.
- The old cloud logo is retired.

Fonts in our materials: IBM Plex Serif SemiBold for headlines, IBM Plex Sans for text. The wordmark itself is fixed artwork (outlined), so it does not change. Full colour scale and type sizes: https://www.npssurveys.com/brand/
