NPS Survey logo kit (CX OS by SalesHub), November 2026

The mark is the Loop: a thick ring with a small gap, closed by one dot. Brand colour: Klein Blue #002FA7 (RGB 0 47 167, CMYK 100 72 0 35 approx; ask the printer to match #002FA7). Text is converted to outlines, so no font is needed.

Which file
- On white or light backgrounds: nps-survey-logo-klein (grey line under) or -klein-mono (all blue)
- On Klein Blue or dark photos: nps-survey-logo-white (or -white-mono); -on-klein has the blue background built in
- One-colour print, fax, engraving: nps-survey-logo-black
- Square spaces (social avatars, slides, booth): the stacked versions
- App icon, favicon, QR code centre: nps-survey-app-icon or the mark files

Formats: svg (web, Figma, Canva, PowerPoint), pdf (print shops, vector), png (transparent, quick use).

Rules
- One flat colour. No gradients, shadows, outlines or effects on the logo.
- Keep clear space around it equal to the dot's diameter x 2. Minimum size: mark 16 px, full logo 120 px wide.
- Don't stretch, rotate, recolour or rearrange it. Don't change the name: "NPS Survey" with "CX OS by SalesHub" under it.
- Fonts in our materials: IBM Plex Serif SemiBold for headlines and big numbers, IBM Plex Sans for text. Full colour scale and type sizes: https://www.npssurveys.com/brand/ (tokens in brand/tokens.css).
