Your survey templates

Four moments that decide your reviews and referrals

Each survey is one or two questions, sent by Salesforce the moment the job reaches that stage. You can change any wording before go-live.

1. Design consultation

CSAT

Sent when the in-home consultation is marked complete.

  1. How satisfied were you with your design consultation? (1 to 5)
  2. Did your consultant explain your options and pricing clearly? (Yes / No)
Then: a 1 or 2 alerts the sales manager the same day, before the homeowner signs elsewhere.

2. Installation day

CSAT

Sent the evening the crew finishes the install.

  1. How would you rate your installation crew? (1 to 5)
  2. Tick all that were true: on time, tidy, respectful, explained how to care for it
Then: results by crew, so you can coach and recognise your best installers.

3. Project complete

NPS

Sent 7 days after the final walkthrough.

  1. How likely are you to recommend Bathworks to a friend or neighbor? (0 to 10)
  2. What's the main reason for your score?
Then: a 9 or 10 gets your Google review link and a referral ask. A 0 to 6 opens a follow-up with an owner and a due date.

4. Warranty or service visit

CES

Sent when a warranty or service case is closed.

  1. How easy was it to get your issue resolved? (1 very easy to 5 very hard)
  2. Is anything still not right? (Yes / No)
Then: a "Yes" reopens the case automatically, so nothing closes until the homeowner is happy.
