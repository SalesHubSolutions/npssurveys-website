Close the loop in seconds

One low score. Four things happen at once.

Best for: Service leaders, CX leaders, account managers

The business problem

A customer gives you a 3. In most teams that answer waits in a report until someone reads it, and by then the customer may already be talking to a competitor.

How it works

  1. The answer lands on the Account, Contact or Case
  2. A detractor rule fires on a score of 0 to 6
  3. A P1 case, an owned follow-up, an email to the exec and a Slack alert go out together
  4. The owner calls, fixes it and closes the loop
What changes: No unhappy answer waits for a weekly review. Each one has an owner and a due time within seconds.
