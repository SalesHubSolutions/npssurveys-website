1. Before you install: turn on Case Feed Tracking

Do this first, or the install will fail. NPS Survey CX OS adds its survey activity to the Case feed, so Salesforce needs Feed Tracking switched on for Cases.

  1. In Salesforce, open Setup.
  2. In Quick Find, type Feed Tracking and open it.
  3. Select Case in the object list.
  4. Tick Enable Feed Tracking and click Save.

Seeing this error?

“Layout(Case-Case Layout) feedLayout is not supported for this layout” means Feed Tracking for Cases is off. Turn it on as above, then install again. Nothing in your org was changed by the failed attempt.

You also need: Salesforce Lightning Experience on Essentials, Professional, Enterprise or Unlimited Edition, a System Administrator login, and a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. People who answer your surveys don’t need a Salesforce login.

  1. Sign in with your System Administrator login.
  2. Choose who gets access. Install for Admins Only is the safe default; you can give others access afterwards.
  3. If Salesforce asks you to approve third-party access to saleshub.lightning.force.com, tick the box and click Continue. That is SalesHub’s own Salesforce org, used for licensing and support.
  4. Click Install. Most installs finish in a few minutes. If Salesforce says it is taking a while, you will get an email when it is done.

Current release: 2.5.5. Installing over an earlier version upgrades it and keeps your surveys and responses.

3. After you install

  1. Open the App Launcher and search for NPS Survey to open the app.
  2. If you installed for Admins Only, give your team access to the app next. We walk you through it on a guided setup call, or email us and we’ll send the steps for your org.
  3. Create your first survey from a template, then send a test to yourself.
